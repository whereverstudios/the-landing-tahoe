## What we collect

When you make a reservation or sign up for offers we collect the details needed to confirm your stay and stay in touch: name, email address, postal code and, for reservations, payment information handled by our booking provider.

## How we use it

To fulfil reservations, respond to requests, and send offers and news you have asked for. You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in any message.

## Your choices

California residents can exercise their rights under the CCPA, including the right to opt out of the sale of personal information, by contacting the resort at 4104 Lakeshore Blvd, South Lake Tahoe, CA 96150.
